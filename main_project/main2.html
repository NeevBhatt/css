My Coding Journey: Building My Digital World


For me, coding is more than just a technical skill; it’s an escape. When I’m deep in a codebase, I feel like a person out of this world. Everything else falls away, leaving only the rhythm of the keyboard and the satisfaction of building something from nothing. It is my creative sanctuary. I am currently channeling that focus into my Personal Portfolio, which serves as my final CSS project. This isn't just a website; it's a reflection of my growth as a developer. One of the most significant hurdles I’ve cleared is mastering CSS Positioning. Learning the nuances between relative, absolute, and fixed positioning felt like solving a complex 3D puzzle. Understanding how to pull elements out of the natural document flow to create unique layouts was the "aha!" moment I needed to take my designs to the next level.
